Stages: Warm-up → Fill in the Blanks → Interactive Reading → Main Idea & Titles → Extra Practice → Wrap-up

- Warm-up: practice test 2 score and hardest tasks, a word from the list with
  two other forms, five spelling-list words spelled aloud, sprints.
- Fill in the Blanks: 4 cards (read the whole sentence, words come in pairs,
  count the boxes, check the grammar). A. Find the partner, 12 rows
  (make/do/take/pay + heavy/strong/high). B. Pairs set (decision, attention,
  reduce, responsibility, traffic, meet). C. Context set (survive, effect,
  solution, evidence, rise, public). Inputs now show one box per missing
  letter (`.fib`, monospace). Sidebar: make/do/take chips.
- Interactive Reading: 3 steps (skim 30 s, keyword then exact words, main idea
  and title last). D. "The four-day week": 5 drop-downs, missing sentence
  (fills the slot in the passage), 2 highlight questions (click the question,
  drag across the passage), main idea, title — one Check, out of 10. Hedged
  that parts can vary on the real test (Complete the Passage is not in the
  outline's task table).
- Main idea & titles: cards Too narrow / Too broad / Not in the text / Just
  right; each option shows its label after Check. E. Volunteering, F.
  Multitasking, G. Food waste (main idea + title each).
- Extra Practice: H. Fill in the Blanks harder (invest, agreement, causes,
  feedback, competition, believed), I. Interactive Reading "City birds", J.
  make your own blanks, K. 5-minute sprint on a four-day week.
- Clock presets: 0:20, 2:00 (6 × 20 s), 1:00, 7:00, 5:00.

**Homework:** daily short article (guess from the title first, main idea in

## Class 4 — Listening Accuracy
`lesson_plan_4.html` · 10 November 2026

Stages: Warm-up → Listen and Type → Check Before You Submit → Interactive Listening → Extra Practice → Wrap-up

- First page with audio: `▶ Play` buttons read lines with the browser's
  speechSynthesis voice (prefers Samantha / Daniel / Karen / Moira, skips
  macOS novelty voices). Play limits mirror the test: 3 plays for Listen and
  Type (official DET blog: 60 s, up to two replays), 1 play per Interactive
  Listening line (official blog: each line heard once, instant feedback). If no
  voice, the button says so and the line is read aloud instead.
- Warm-up: daily article main idea + word pair, word with its collocation,
  sprints' linking words and practice test 2, what's hardest when listening.
- Listen and Type: cards (small words go quiet, contractions — type what you
  hear, endings, use the three plays). A. Hear the small words, 8 sentences /
  16 one-word gaps (a, of, have, for, a, do, to, that, had, were, of, at, of,
  the, has, this), same-size boxes (`.open-gaps`). B. Everyday, C. Academic
  (5 sentences each, `LT` data). Whole sentences checked word by word (LCS),
  case and punctuation ignored, but a missing capital/full stop gets a note;
  Show answers marks missed words in the key and lists what was typed instead.
- Check before you submit: table (verb + s, past tense, plural, have/has,
  a/the/of, start and end). D. Fix the typing, 7 prefilled wrong sentences
  (one mistake each, 1 play, 0:30).
- Interactive Listening: 3 steps (read the situation, answer the last line,
  3-sentence summary). Each conversation (`IL` data): situation, Listen and
  complete (3 gaps in the first line), 4–5 reply turns with instant feedback and
  a reason on every option (Fits / Too rude / Off topic / Same word, different
  meaning…), summary box + model summary. E. Office hours (essay extension),
  F. Group project. Hedged: parts can vary; 75 s summary is from prep sites only.
- Extra Practice: G. Listen and Type harder, H. Interactive Listening "Library
  desk", I. Antonio dictates 3 sentences to Felipe, J. sprint (listening vs
  reading) + 1:15 self-summary.
- Clock presets: 0:30, 1:00, 2:00, 4:00, 1:15, 5:00.

**Homework:** daily 5–10 min video/podcast, 2 sentences exactly as heard (max 3
plays, check with subtitles, circle misses, send 2) · word list, now listened
to in a dictionary first · spelling list adds today's misses · sprints + a
75-second three-sentence summary of each, 7 new prompts, send 2 · practice
test 2 only if still not taken.

## Class 5 — Speaking: Photos & Prompts
`lesson_plan_5.html` · 12 November 2026

Stages: Warm-up → Speak About the Photo → Read / Listen, Then Speak → Listen Back & Fix → Extra Practice → Wrap-up

- First page with an in-page recorder (`.rec`): Start runs a 20 s prep
  countdown, beeps, then records 1:30 by itself (Stop unlocks after 0:30, Skip
  prep, Cancel during prep). Each take: audio, Save (download), a strip of
  voice vs. pauses of 2+ s (click to jump), and a line "1:30 recorded · voice
  until 1:24 · 2 pauses of 2+ seconds". Nothing is uploaded. Without a mic the
  timer still runs and says to record on the phone.
- Warm-up: listening sentences and missed small words, a word said as the
  dictionary said it, a 75-second summary, practice test 2, recording himself.
- Speak About the Photo: photo frame as an apply-box (toolkit wording), cards
  (-ing for actions, never "I don't know", go round twice, bigger words).
  A. Which part of the frame? (8 sentences about the park photo: Overview /
  Detail / Story / Personal link). B. the park out loud, C. the station
  (delayed 08:15, clock 8:20, bench, newspaper). Model answers, with ▶ Hear it.
- Read / Listen, Then Speak: cards (three keywords, no full sentences, pick a
  side fast, bullets are a plan) + opinion frame. D. Pick the best notes
  (4 prompts × 3 note sets, reason on each). E. Read Then Speak, place to
  recommend (3 keyword boxes). F. Listen Then Speak, phones (1 play, hedged:
  check play count on the practice test).
- Listen back & fix: table (long silence, restarts, um/uh, same word, stopping
  before 1:00, same sentence starts). G. Swap the safe word (spacious,
  exhausted, terrible, crowded, delighted, stunning, an excellent, freezing;
  distractors are wrong form or wrong meaning). H. Record it twice (daily
  routine), two fixes between takes.
- Extra Practice: I. the market photo, J. prompt deck (8 prompts), K. his own
  phone photo while Felipe sketches, L. sprint (speaking vs writing) then say
  it with the text hidden.
- Clock presets: 0:20, 1:00, 1:30, 5:00.

**Homework:** one 90-second recording a day (20 s, 3 keywords, record,
listen back for pauses/repeats/one safe word, record again), 7 prompts, send 2
first + second takes · word list, use 2 list words in each recording ·
listening continues, retell it as the recording on prompt 2 · 3 sprints, on
the previous day's prompt · practice test 2 only if still not taken.
