## Class 2 — Real Words & Missing Letters
`lesson_plan_2.html` · 3 November 2026 (moved from 29 October)

Stages: Warm-up → Real or Fake? → Missing Letters → Spelling Traps → Extra Practice → Wrap-up

- Warm-up: practice test 2 score and hardest tasks, 3 words from his list used
  aloud, best sprint word count.
- Real or fake: 4 cards (wrong ending, misspelled real word, sounds English but
  means nothing, pick only what you know). A. Build the word (8 word-family
  items: decision, carefully, successful, development, responsibility,
  unbelievable, generosity, increased). B. Read and Select, 3 rounds (everyday,
  academic, misspellings). Sidebar: prefixes, noun and adjective endings.
- Missing letters: 3 steps (first sentence, small words first, grammar for the
  rest), a grammar-clue table (plural, 3rd-person -s, tense, noun/adjective,
  comparative, -ly). C. Read and Complete "Sleep", D. "Cities" (16 gaps each).
- Spelling traps: E. 16 right/wrong pairs (necessary, environment, receive,
  separate, definitely, government, beginning, occurred, believe, argument,
  accommodate, until, tomorrow, business, successful, which) + 5 tips. Start a
  personal spelling list.
- Extra Practice stage (added on the day, as a reserve): F. Read and Select
  rounds 4–5 (hard academic, tricky spelling), G. Read and Complete "Online
  learning", H. "Coffee", I. make your own fakes, J. live 5-minute writing
  sprint on whether spelling still matters.
- First lesson with the shared clock moved between stages (1:00 / 2:00 / 3:00).

Fake words used: wisdomful, journeal, frequid, neighbent, borrence, threatment,

enviroment, goverment, untill, occurence, arguement, aquaintance, concience, rythm, priviledge, maintainance, garantee, occasionaly.

**Homework:** practice test 2 if not done yet · word list continues, now with
two other forms of each word · spelling list, 5 from memory daily · daily sprint

## Class 3 — Reading Strategies
`lesson_plan_3.html` · 5 November 2026

Stages: Warm-up → Fill in the Blanks → Interactive Reading → Main Idea & Titles → Extra Practice → Wrap-up

- Warm-up: practice test 2 score and hardest tasks, a word from the list with
  two other forms, five spelling-list words spelled aloud, sprints.
- Fill in the Blanks: 4 cards (read the whole sentence, words come in pairs,
  count the boxes, check the grammar). A. Find the partner, 12 rows
  (make/do/take/pay + heavy/strong/high). B. Pairs set (decision, attention,
  reduce, responsibility, traffic, meet). C. Context set (survive, effect,
  solution, evidence, rise, public). Inputs now show one box per missing
  letter (`.fib`, monospace). Sidebar: make/do/take chips.
- Interactive Reading: 3 steps (skim 30 s, keyword then exact words, main idea
  and title last). D. "The four-day week": 5 drop-downs, missing sentence
  (fills the slot in the passage), 2 highlight questions (click the question,
  drag across the passage), main idea, title — one Check, out of 10. Hedged
  that parts can vary on the real test (Complete the Passage is not in the
  outline's task table).
- Main idea & titles: cards Too narrow / Too broad / Not in the text / Just
  right; each option shows its label after Check. E. Volunteering, F.
  Multitasking, G. Food waste (main idea + title each).
- Extra Practice: H. Fill in the Blanks harder (invest, agreement, causes,
  feedback, competition, believed), I. Interactive Reading "City birds", J.
  make your own blanks, K. 5-minute sprint on a four-day week.
- Clock presets: 0:20, 2:00 (6 × 20 s), 1:00, 7:00, 5:00.

**Homework:** daily short article (guess from the title first, main idea in
one sentence, two word pairs) · word list now with its collocation · spelling
list, 5 from memory · sprints with 2+ linking words, 7 new prompts, send 2 ·
practice test 2 only if still not taken.
