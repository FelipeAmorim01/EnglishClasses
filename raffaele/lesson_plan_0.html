More precise ways to talk about your work
Instead of Try this Example
"I do marketing" I am responsible for... "I am responsible for managing our social media campaigns."
"I work with the team" I collaborate with... "I collaborate closely with the sales and design teams."
"My job is..." My role involves... "My role involves overseeing client accounts."
"I am in charge of..." I oversee / I lead... "I oversee a team of five people."
"I want to learn more" I'm looking to develop... "I'm looking to develop my skills in project management."
Stage 03 of 04
CEO for a Day
You are the CEO. Every decision is yours to make — and defend.
The scenario

You have just been appointed CEO of a company. The board has called an emergency meeting. Four urgent issues are on the table. What do you do?

Your challenges
Challenge 01
Revenue is falling
  • What is your first move — cut costs or find new revenue?
  • Which department do you protect? Which do you cut?
  • How do you communicate this to your team without causing panic?
Challenge 02
Employees want remote work
  • Do you allow it? Fully, partially, or not at all?
  • How do you keep the team aligned from different places?
  • Your best manager says they'll quit if remote is not allowed. What do you do?
Challenge 03
A competitor launched a new product
  • Do you respond immediately or wait and observe?
  • Do you match the product or double down on what makes you different?
  • How do you keep your current clients from switching?
Challenge 04
The budget is limited
  • You can only invest in one area: marketing, technology, or people. Which one?
  • A promised project now has no funding. How do you handle it?
  • An investor offers money but wants 30% of the company. Do you accept?
Useful language for this activity
Making decisions
I would prioritize... The first thing I'd do is... In the long run... We need to allocate resources to...
Arguing your position
The reason behind this is... The risk here is... This could lead to... On the other hand...
Finding middle ground
I'm open to... That's not negotiable, but... We need to find a middle ground What if we tried...
